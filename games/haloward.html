Follow development What is Haloward?

The war on the seam

Two realms pressing against each other, and one thin line between them. You decide what holds it.

Haloward is a tower defense game built around a single tension: every defence you raise belongs either to the light or to the fire, and the two do not mix for free.

Lean holy and your towers are steady, precise and slow to break — but they cost you tempo. Lean infernal and you get raw damage and speed, paid for in instability. Most runs are decided by how far you let the balance tip, and when you tip it back.

Two ways to hold a line

The Celestial

Order, reach and protection. Slow to set up, hard to break.

  • Sustained damage and long sight lines
  • Shields and cleansing that undo enemy effects
  • Buffs that make neighbouring towers stronger

The Infernal

Burst, spread and pressure. Cheap and fast, never stable.

  • Burn and chain damage that scales with crowds
  • Sacrifice mechanics — spend what you have to spike output
  • Corruption that spreads to the ground around it

What you'll be doing

  • Balance as a resource

    The seam shifts as you build. Push too far either way and the map answers.

  • Towers that combine

    Holy and infernal towers placed together unlock hybrid behaviour — at a price.

  • Handmade maps

    Every map is authored, not generated, with its own read on the war.

  • Runs, not campaigns

    Short, replayable sessions. Lose a line, learn it, take it again.
